Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At Deeksha Jewellers, your privacy matters. This policy explains what information we handle when you use our website, call us or message us on WhatsApp.

1. Information We Collect

This website has no accounts, no forms and no online payments — so we do not collect any information through the website itself. When you choose to contact us by call or WhatsApp at +91 70090 81860, you voluntarily share details such as your name, phone number, messages, design preferences and order-related information. We only see what you choose to send us.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you share solely to respond to your enquiries, provide quotes and guidance, craft and deliver your orders, and keep you updated about your order. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.

3. WhatsApp & Third-Party Services

Our WhatsApp buttons open WhatsApp, a service operated by Meta. Messages you send through WhatsApp are also governed by WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy. Similarly, our call buttons use your device’s phone dialler. We have no control over those third-party services.

4. Cookies & Tracking

This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics or advertising tools. It is a simple showcase website — your visit stays on your device.

5. Data Security & Retention

We treat your personal information with care and keep it only as long as needed to serve your enquiries and orders, or as required by law. We take reasonable precautions to protect the information you share with us.

6. Your Rights

You may ask us at any time to correct or delete the personal information you have shared with us. Simply message or call us at +91 70090 81860 and we will be happy to help.

7. Children

Our jewellery and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, along with the date it was last updated.

9. Contact Us

For any questions about this policy, call or WhatsApp us at +91 70090 81860.
